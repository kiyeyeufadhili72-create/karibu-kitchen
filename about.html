About KARIBU KITCHEN

Problem: Many restaurants in Dar es Salaam have no online menu or online booking. Customers must call or visit to know prices.

Solution: KARIBU KITCHEN website lets customers view menu with pictures and prices, search food, filter by category, add to cart, and make a reservation online with validation.

Objectives

  • Provide online menu with pictures and prices
  • Allow searching and filtering of food
  • Allow reservation with form validation
  • Provide cart that calculates total price

Intended Users

Students, families, workers and tourists in Dar es Salaam.
